# Commit plan: the walk

## Summary — the state after all commits

Every manual reads to the shape and against the conventions
manual, which was born yesterday with nine derivatives and has not
yet walked them. Each of the nine has the eight sections where a
reader expects them: a header saying what derives from it and how
a disagreement ends, an opening statement, *what it is for* written
from its own birth story, *what ships*, both seats named, dated
italics for what was measured, *what this does not cover* as a
list by path, and *what this is made usable as*. No subject is
stated in two manuals: six that were have one home each, and the
other places point at it by path. Two contradictions between
manuals are decided and gone. Every fact stale since 2026-09-27 is
current, every link resolves, and "this repo" means the deliverer
by that word wherever it means that. The first firing of the rule
for every description on a newborn description is done, and the
TODO items for it and for the kit wording close.

The set changes no shipped file. The manuals never ship.

## Decisions taken inside this plan

- **The two contradictions are decided here, not in an ADR.** The
  entry-file address is a fact about the container (it ships
  `.claude/CLAUDE.md`; ADR-0035 decision 5 records the move as a
  pure rename), so project-recording's diagram is wrong and is
  corrected. The edit-in-place question is ADR-0036's, decided; the
  hygiene manual predates it and follows.
- **Homes for the six duplicates**, one each, the other places
  pointing: the micro-ADR sentence to commit-messages (it is about
  the body); the measured case to agent-arrangement (it is the
  entry file's failure); the moment rule to project-recording §13,
  with agent-arrangement keeping only its test for what enters the
  entry file; the shapes directory as the force to shapes;
  the commit stop to commit-messages; copies downstream of the
  container to the exchange's fact 3, the seats paragraphs saying
  "a copy" and pointing.
- **The exchange keeps its first person inside the deliverer's
  sections.** §3 and §6 are written from our chair and say so
  once; rewriting them in the third person would be a change of
  voice, not a correction. Outside those sections "this repo"
  becomes the deliverer like everywhere else.
- **Adopted is an honest "what it is for".** Two manuals came from
  the handbook with no failure of ours recorded. Their section
  says so, and names what has been lived under them since; it does
  not invent a failure, and it does not call itself a trial that
  was never opened.
- **The master states no rule, and the headers stop citing it for
  one** (revision at step 2's boundary). 4a left the decline
  paragraph in the master's §3 and every manual header pointing
  there; the first header written to the shape copied the pointer.
  Fixed in the walk because the walk is where the nine headers are
  being written, and deferred it would be written nine times on
  purpose.
- **Root paths only.** The pointer paragraph allowed relative
  links between manuals on the argument that they carry the same
  grep token. They do, and they still read differently from every
  other root; the rule is simpler with one form. Every step writes
  root paths, and step 10 tightens the paragraph.
- **One list of what is made usable, not three sections naming
  the same files** (found by the form test, step 2). *What ships*
  was the run-facing answer near the top; *made usable as* was the
  walk list at the end; the seats named the files again. The list
  moves up and carries both jobs; the seats keep only the doing.
- **Thinnest first.** commit-plan tests the form on 74 lines
  before it is applied to 577. If the form costs more than it
  gives on the small one, the plan is revised before the large
  ones.
