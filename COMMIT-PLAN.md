## Decisions for you to object to

- **By layer, not by finding.** Round 1's close read worked its 39
  fixes in four commits. Here 50 fixes go in ten, so each diff stays
  one manual or one group, and reverts alone.
- **Cross-layer findings land whole in their first layer,** so a
  fix is made once: 236 and 238 in project-recording's commit.
- **The collapse is its own commit,** after the endings name their
  commits, as `a629f52` was round 1's.
- **No plan revision for the fixes' wording.** Each fix is the
  proposed ending you took; a fix that turns out to need more is
  shown at its boundary.
- **PLAN's Now line stays** until round 3's rule check opens.
