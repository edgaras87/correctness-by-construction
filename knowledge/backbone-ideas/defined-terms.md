This document says what a Defined Term is, and what keeps its meaning exact wherever it is read. It names no Work Domain: each Work Domain Idea adapted from it says how its texts mark, define and share their Defined Terms.

## 1. What a Defined Term is

A **Defined Term** is a word, or words, given an exact meaning that plain English does not give them. It is written so a reader tells it apart from plain words at every use, before reading its definition. Plain words then stay free to mean what English means.

A reader meets a word and takes it as English, unless something shows it is not. A meaning held only in the writer's head is read as English, and drifts. So a Defined Term is marked, and its meaning is written down.

**Rules:**

- A Defined Term is coined only when plain words cannot say the thing.
- It is written exactly the same at every use. A changed word is read as another thing.

## 2. One meaning per Defined Term

A reader who meets one Defined Term with two meanings merges them into one, and cannot tell which was meant. A reader who meets two Defined Terms for one thing takes them as two things.

**Rules:**

- One Defined Term has one meaning, and one meaning has one Defined Term. A short form of a Defined Term counts as a Defined Term here: it never shares its words with another.
- Before a new Defined Term is defined, it is checked against those already defined, where its texts may be read together.

## 3. Where it is defined

A definition written in two texts drifts: one is changed, and the other is not. A reader of the other then holds the old meaning.

**Rules:**

- A Defined Term is defined in one text. Every other text that uses it points there, and never retells the definition.
- A definition names what it builds on by its Defined Term, never by where it stands in the text, such as "the same idea" or "the two".

## 4. Plain words

A plain word may be given a narrow sense in a set of texts, such as "the user" for one kind of person. A reader takes it as English, and the narrow sense passes unseen into other texts read with it.

**Rules:**

- A plain word kept in a narrow sense is written down with that sense, where its Defined Terms are defined.
- Such words are kept as few as can be.
