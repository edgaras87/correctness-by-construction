# Document header

**Governs:** how the opening block of any document here is written.
Its form, never its content.

A document opens by saying what it is, in its own words. Four
things, and the last two are what change how it gets treated:

- **What this is** — a sentence or two.
- **Read it** — who opens it, and when.
- **Ignoring it owes an explanation** — yes or no.
- **Goes stale** — yes, and what makes it lie; or no.

The block is written for this document rather than chosen from a
vocabulary of kinds. To ask for a new document like an existing one,
name the existing one: its block says what is being asked for.

Where the block sits is the document's own business — an HTML
comment for a record whose reader is us, visible prose for one whose
reader is a stranger.
