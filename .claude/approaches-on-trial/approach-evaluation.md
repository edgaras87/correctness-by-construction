# Approach evaluation

**On trial.** How an approach on trial is judged, and its pieces
placed where they belong. The deliverer's alone: a run hands its
approaches over and never evaluates. The part a run holds is
`.claude/approaches-on-trial/approach-drafting.md`.

## What it is for

So that what a piece of work taught goes where it belongs, piece by
piece, and nothing unproven is locked into a convention. Before
this, first-time work's close made one lasting form, chosen at once
(ADR-0056). The redesign of 2026-10-19 closed as three pieces, which
one form could not say. The refinement's draft was counted as a
manual before anyone judged whether it was one.

## The stages of an approach on trial

1. **Drafted:** during its first use, specific to that work, by
   `approach-drafting`.
2. **On trial:** rewritten in general at its first evaluation. Each
   later use adds a dated note and is evaluated again.
3. **Placed or dropped:** a piece is placed into a convention when
   it meets what its approach named would settle it, or dropped at
   any evaluation. The approach file goes when nothing is left in it.

## When it opens

- **A drafted approach is handed over.** Ours, at the close of
  `approach-drafting`. A run's, when a reading meets its offer line
  under *To the deliverer*.
- **An approach on trial has a new dated note** from a later use,
  ours or a run's.

Placing is not a third moment: it is the "placed" ending, reached at
either.

## Stages

1. **Read the approach whole,** and its history: one commit per
   lesson, against what the work did. For a run's, the commits its
   offer line names.
2. **Split it into pieces,** one subject each: a procedure, a rule,
   a form of output, a fact.
3. **Find each piece's home.** An existing convention first: a
   section of its manual, or a section to add to it. A new
   convention only when none fits. A fact may belong in a record.
4. **Propose an ending for each piece,** with the reason. The
   reviewer decides each:
   - **placed:** written into its home now;
   - **on trial:** kept, and rewritten in general;
   - **the project's own:** it stays with the project that drafted
     it, and goes no further;
   - **dropped:** nothing in it is worth keeping.
5. **Place what is placed,** as a commit plan. A new convention is
   added by `docs/conventions/conventions/` §4. A section of an
   existing one goes into its manual first, and the foundation
   check carries it to what derives from it.
6. **Rewrite what stays on trial, in general.** The general
   statement comes first, in its own terms. Each use stands beside
   it as a dated note, as a lesson does in a manual
   (`docs/conventions/conventions/` §3.7). The approach names what
   would settle it. If a whole part is used by one seat alone, it
   splits (below).
7. **Record the endings.** For our own approach, an ADR when a
   convention is made or changed and options were weighed. For a
   run's, the reading's note carries each ending.

## Seats

- **An approach splits by seat only when a whole part is used by one
  seat alone.** That part becomes an approach of its own, and the
  other seat never receives it. This one split from
  `approach-drafting` that way.
- **A step that differs is marked inline** in the shared approach:
  "At the deliverer:", "In a run:". Not "here", which means the
  reader's own seat.
- **Each idea lives in the part that acts on it.** The stages and
  the seats are here because only an evaluation acts on them.
- **Our part points at the shared one by its path.** The shared one
  says the deliverer holds a part that does not ship, and gives no
  path: a run cannot open it.
- What ships to a run is `docs/conventions/exchange/` §3.2's.

## The reviewer's reading is the check

This approach judges approaches, itself and `approach-drafting`
among them. The agent will tend to find its own approach good. So
the agent proposes, the reviewer decides each ending, and no
evaluation closes on the agent's word.

## Not known yet

- **Whether a manual still goes on trial.** A convention could be
  adopted marked on trial, as first-time work was. Now unproven work
  stays in approaches, and the mark may no longer be needed.
- **Whether each approach's "settled when" is enough,** or placing
  needs a floor of uses.
- **Whether this and `approach-drafting` become one convention**
  when placed: one manual, a skill for each seat.
- **What a run does with a piece left as the project's own:** keeps
  it as its approach on trial, or writes it as a skill or rule of
  its own (`docs/conventions/agent-arrangement/` §3.1).
- **Whether generalizing is work of its own.** Today it is stage 6.
  Both are the deliverer's, so no seat splits them; a first use
  that shows it big enough splits it.
- **How a run's offered approach reaches this evaluation.** Only
  *When it opens* says so. Weighed on 2026-10-20: a skill opening
  when a reading meets the offer line; `working-a-reading.md`
  pointing here; or nothing, the reading working it by hand.
