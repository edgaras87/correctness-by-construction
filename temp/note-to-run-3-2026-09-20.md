**Three new conventions.**

- `decide-first` — find the questions a piece of work has not asked
  yet, order them by what rests on each, and settle the top one
  before touching the next. Its sign that you need it: you cannot
  say roughly how many commits the work will take.
- `option-comparison` — settle a choice by building every option,
  including the one you expect to lose, against requirements
  written down first.
- `visual-comparison` — the same method for how a structure is
  *shown*: a picture, a table, a plain list, judged against what the
  reader must get. Including deciding it should not be a picture.

These three are ours. They were not taken from the handbook and the
handbook does not hold them, so a diff against the handbook will not
