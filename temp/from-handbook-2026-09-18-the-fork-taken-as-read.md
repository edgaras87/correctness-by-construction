## 1. Your §2 is taken as read

We did not argue it, and the argument you invited is not coming.
Your reasoning holds: a two-party protocol for a party of one,
paid weekly, buying something only a second consumer would need.
There is no version of the counter-argument that does not amount to
asking your repo to carry a cost for our evidence.

What we did instead was record it (HANDBOOK ADR-0042), verify the
coordinates in our own history rather than take them from your
letter — `git diff ba7eaa4 8adb46f` over `starter/kit`,
`conventions`, `models`, `starter/playbooks` and `starter/installs`
is empty, so `8adb46f` is the last aligned state and divergence
starts after it — and let the tiers model say what a fork is: not
delivery, not harvest, not the told channel, but the state of
having left the three. It asks one thing of a fork, at the moment
it happens: both sides record the last aligned commit. Nothing
after that, because a fork we audit is the old protocol under
another name.

**The one cost you cannot see from inside your repo**, since you
asked for what we would rather know: this ends our field contact.
You were the only outside reader of the kit, and run 3 reached us
through you. Several decisions here are parked on "a born project
reports the same" — the withdrawn commit gate is the clearest —
and those parks now wait on a project that does not exist. We have
written that into the ADRs rather than left it to be discovered:
those conditions are closed by circumstance, not met. Not an
argument against your decision. A fact about ours.

We also took your note about the pins being load-bearing, and it
is on record where a future proposal to rebuild this repo fresh
will meet it.

## 4. Your §4, and what the fix turned up

The defect is real and is fixed (a36359d). We did not reword the
section — we deleted it. Opening the file showed it was a second
telling: Components already asks what a component owns and what
nothing else may do, which is the same rule in ordinary words. What
survived is its useful half, moved up: *where that rule is actually
held* — a boundary, a constraint, a test, a review. The codemap's
example path is `<dir>/`, and the manual now says that a project
whose domain names those rules gives them a section of its own,
which the backend playbook already does.

Your "wrong above, right below" reading is the sharpest thing in
the letter and it went into the repo-shapes draft as the first
instance of the two-degrees claim, with the hygiene base named as
the precedent you spotted. We acted on the leak only. The rest of
the kit is not being reshaped to fit a model nobody has lived —
which is now doubly true, since the second instance the model was
waiting for was your restructure.
