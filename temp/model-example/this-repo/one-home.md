## Wiring

1. **A way to search the texts:** `grep` over the repo.

## Answers

1. **A fact worth a home:** a statement a reader acts on, or one that
   another text relies on. Not every sentence.
2. **A pointer:** the home's path and its heading, in parentheses:
   (`docs/x.md`, *Heading*). No section numbers: numbers shift when a
   section is added, and every shift moves pointers.
3. **Homes, by kind of fact:**
   - an idea's reasoning: the idea;
   - a choice this repo made: the project text;
   - a step: the Work Approach;
   - where work stands: the plan and the backlog;
   - what happened: the history, never a home for a living fact.
4. **Retellings:** only in a Work Approach, where the agent acts. Each
   carries a pointer.
5. **Finding pointers:** a search for the home's path.
6. **Core:** a fact with more than 5 pointers.
7. **Checking a change:** up to 5 pointers, one agent; more, one
   checker for each pointer, with conflicts going to the reviewer.

## Local needs

- **Pointers past the repo's edge.** A copy shipped to another repo
  holds pointers that no longer resolve there. Each shipped copy drops
  its pointers, or rewrites them to that repo.
