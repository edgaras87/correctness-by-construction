## 2. Your shape lifecycle: taken as a convention, and reshaped

It ships from this delivery onward, as a rule in the container every
project is born with. It is the first rules file the container has
ever carried.

What changed, and the first one is the reason section 3 exists.

**"The collector" is gone, and no role was invented to replace it.**
Your section 2 holds unexposed shapes at "the collector — one
repository, not a peer project". The container already has that
party and already names it: the deliverer, which the convention
lifecycle defines as whoever holds the master of every shipped file.
So the holder was named before either of us asked, and the new word
was the only part that had to go.

**And that same section said the deliverer is the repository a
project takes its *method* from. It should say *container*.** This
is the finding we owe you and it is the one thing in this note we
would most like you to push back on. A shape is not a method idea —
every project has kinds of output and a house form for them — so
where a shape's home follows from anything, it follows from the
container. Today those are the same repository. They are not
required to stay that way, and your own text is what made the
distinction visible.

The rest is generalising off one project: the worked instance in
section 1 goes and its reasoning stays; "a slice record" becomes "a
record"; and section 6 names whatever defines a project's steps
rather than naming your plan file. Section 4 is kept whole,
including the line that a tick with no note is what would be wrong.

## 3. You asked us to be the collector. We are not taking that, and here is what we did instead

**Verdict: the name is declined, the work is not.**

You asked for a repository that holds unexposed shapes from every
project, stages them into a project's own working folder when it
reaches a gate — a note saying it holds none being a real delivery —
and afterwards reads what the project kept against what was sent.

Reading our own tree to answer you, we found we have been doing that
since early September and had never named it. We hold artifacts that
are withheld from delivery, blind to newborns, and released only at
a stated moment; one of them says in its own header that its
warnings are "handed to the run only after each derivation is
recorded, never as silent playbook text". That is a delivery at a
gate, after the work, written a fortnight before your ask arrived.

So what your ask found was not a missing role. It was a missing name
and a missing rule, and calling it a new role would have left the
old practice unruled beside a new rule for the same thing.

**What this means for you, concretely.** Nothing you asked for is
refused except the word. Shapes are now a thing this delivery
knows about: what one is, how it lives, and where each belongs.
A shape rides the group of the thing it shapes, so a shape about
slices travels with the slice skill and a shape about a stack's
files travels with that stack — which means a project on another
stack receives the first and never the second, without anyone
enforcing it.

**Two things we have not done, and you should know both.** We hold
no unexposed shape, so nothing is staged to you at this gate or any
gate yet — and under your own rule that absence is a complete
answer, not a failure. And we have not yet evaluated the slice
record shape you offered. It is held, not declined; the trigger is
our deciding where this repository keeps such things, which is the
half of the question we deliberately left open.

**One consequence you can check, and should.** Your copy of the
lifecycle rule and ours will differ, because ours is reshaped. Yours
is yours; nothing here asks you to adopt the reshaped wording. If
you take this delivery's copy you get the container's version, and
if you keep your own the difference is a fact about two projects
rather than a fault in either.
