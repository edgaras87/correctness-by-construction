## Decisions

- **D1** — the commit type for a new agent skill here, `feat(agent)` or
  `chore(agent)`, and whether our convention skills are copies of the
  container's or derivations. The reviewer's. Rests on it: F21, F22.
  **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-01: `chore(agent)` for a new
  skill too, in the shipped skill, so both seats stay identical and
  what we ship is what we do — a skill an agent holds is no feature
  a release tool should count. Rejected: keeping `feat(agent)` and
  following it here; letting our copy differ from the container's.
  And derivations, as ADR-0042 already decided: the three manuals
  that say "a copy" take its words.
- **D2** — whether the partial reversals of 2026-09-24 need an ADR of
  their own. Rests on it: F62.
- **D3** — F7: what counts as run history in a shipped file.
  Proposed: a run's name, its ordinal, and a story of what one run
  did go — a run cannot resolve "run 3", and run 3 least of all,
  since the ordinal is ours. What stays is evidence strength without
  a name — "lived twice", "lived once" — which tells the reader how
  far to lean on a rule. The reviewer's. **Settled** by the reviewer,
  2026-10-01, as proposed.
- **D4** — F18: `cbc-slice`'s gate drops "key" from its list of leaked
  mechanisms, as the workflow's gate already does, or narrows it to a
  key we invent. Proposed: drop it; a key the caller sends is the
  door's contract, and the list's "any technology" still catches a
  mechanism. The reviewer's. **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-01,
  as proposed.
- **D5** — F15: the worked example takes only the audience fix, or
  also the four outputs it predates — what done demonstrably means,
  the runtime ground, the fence list, the not-probed ledger. Proposed:
  all, a line or two each, in both byte-identical copies; the skill
  sends a framer to the example "whenever unsure what a step's output
  looks like", so an example missing a required output teaches the
  gap. The reviewer's. **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-01, as
  proposed, and extended: the framing workflow's header gains a
  guard, where a rule is changed — when a step's required output
  changes, the example changes in the same commit. Removing the example for rules alone was weighed and
  declined: run 3 found a worked example with real numbers is what
  made its records readable where labelled rules were not.
- **D6** — F30: every shipped file carries `foundation`, said in the
  conventions manual three times and in exchange §3.5's heading;
  36 shipped files carry none — references, templates, stubs,
  dotfiles. Proposed: the claim narrows to every shipped skill and
  rule, as ARCHITECTURE §5 already says — a template in SQL or YAML
  cannot carry frontmatter, a reference belongs to its skill, and a
  stub is the run's own from birth. The reviewer's. **Settled** by the
  reviewer, 2026-10-02, as proposed.
- **D7** — F33: exchange §7, *What this replaced*, a history table in
  the body naming `master.md`, which is gone. Proposed: it goes, a
  line pointing at CBC ADR-0036, which records every row. The
  reviewer's. **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-02, as proposed.
- **D8** — F37: the shapes manual's §4 and §5 lead parts with bold
  labels. Proposed: a bold lead that opens a part of its own becomes
  `### 4.N` or `### 5.N` — at least *Why kept rather than deleted*,
  *Staleness, honestly*, *Birth and delivery, drawn* and *Use,
  drawn*; the bold claims beside them (*A project fetches nothing*,
  *two separable layers*, *the dashed lines back*, *a shape record
  that never changes*) are read in place at the build and each
  decided part or emphasis. The reviewer's. **Settled** by the
  reviewer, 2026-10-02, as proposed.
- **D9** — F48: CHANGELOG's *Unreleased* tells changes since v1 that
  were themselves undone before any release — two entry-file
  templates deleted, the semi-pure step dissolved, `starter/` paths,
  "every delivery a commit on main" where a birth uses a receipt
  branch — under two *Changed* headings. Proposed: rewrite it as the
  net change since v1, one heading per kind, every line true today;
  what came and went is git's and the ADRs'. The reviewer's.
  **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-02, as proposed, and
  extended: the CHANGELOG's header comment gains the rule whose
  absence let it drift — Unreleased is the net change since the last
  release, and a later change that undoes or reshapes an Unreleased
  entry edits that entry rather than adding one.
- **D10** — F54, F68: how this repo's own text cites its decisions.
  Proposed: the rule as written — bare in everything that stays here,
  ARCHITECTURE and the manuals swept; the tag only in what ships.
  Rejected, proposed: writing the manuals' practice into the rule,
  a reason invented after the fact; fixing ARCHITECTURE alone and
  holding the manuals. Baselines stay as frozen. The reviewer's.
  **Settled** by the reviewer, 2026-10-02, as proposed: no tag kept
  for an upstream that does not exist. If a maintainer repo comes,
  what moves there is retagged in that move — the trigger already
  held in TODO, filtering a maintainer's container out of this
